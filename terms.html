Privacy disclosure

This is an educational browser-permissions demonstration. Camera and location access are optional and are requested only after you acknowledge the disclosure and press the relevant button.

1. About this website

This website provides a calculator and demonstrates how websites can request browser permissions and access information that a visitor chooses to share.

2. Camera access

  • Camera access is requested only when you press “Enable camera preview”.
  • If allowed, the live preview appears on your device.
  • This local version does not record, upload, or transmit camera footage.
  • You can stop the camera at any time using the “Stop camera” button or by closing the page.

3. Location access

  • Location access is requested only when you press “Share my location”.
  • If allowed, your browser may provide latitude, longitude, and an accuracy estimate.
  • This local version displays those coordinates on your device and does not upload them to a server.
  • You can deny the request or revoke the website's location permission in your browser settings.

4. Information visible in this version

The page can display the permission result and the location coordinates you explicitly request it to obtain. This version does not have a remote administrator dashboard and does not send camera or location data to a maker or administrator.

5. Device and browser information

Websites may be able to read limited browser and device information. This demo does not send that information to an external service.

6. Data storage and transmission

This is a local-only version. It does not upload the camera feed or location coordinates, and it does not create remote session records. If a future version adds server-side collection, its disclosure and consent flow must be updated before that feature is used.

7. Your choices

You may decline either permission, stop the camera preview, avoid sharing location, or close the website. Browser permission can also be changed later in browser settings.

8. Educational purpose

This project is intended to teach privacy awareness and explain browser permissions. It must not be used to monitor people without their knowledge and informed consent.
